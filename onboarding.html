Here's how Pinn works

Three things to know and you're set.

1

Summon it anytime

Press ⌘ Shift P (or Ctrl Shift P on Windows) from anywhere. Pinn appears instantly on top of everything.

2

Screenshot & ask

Tap the camera icon to grab a screenshot of your screen. Ask Pinn what it sees — great for debugging, reading, summarizing.

3

Invisible by design

No dock icon, no Cmd+Tab entry. Screen recording won't show it. Close with Esc or the shortcut — gone instantly.
