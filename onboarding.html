Here's how Pinn works

Three things to know and you're set.

1

Summon it anytime

Press ⌘ Shift P from anywhere. Pinn floats on top of everything — invisible to screen recordings.

2

Screenshot & ask

Tap the camera icon to grab any area of your screen. Ask Pinn what it sees — great for debugging, reading, summarizing.

3

Dismiss instantly

Press ⌘ Shift P again or click ✕ to hide. No dock icon, no trace. Like it was never there.
